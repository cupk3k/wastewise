WasteWise Desa Mentari · CSPH Group 1

Happy-path tests (HP1–HP13)

wastewise-desa-mentari.vercel.app

Start: do HP13 first (add the app to the home screen) and run every other test in the app opened from its icon. Then gear icon → Reset demo data → OK. The app is on Blok 6, in English.

HP1. Home and announcements

Plan: home feed with announcements; community activities

  1. Open the app. Yellow banner, "Next collection" board tagged "Sample schedule".
  2. Tap the Gotong-royong announcement. When, Where, "Sample" tag.
  3. Tap back. Home again.

HP2. Collection schedule

Plan: collection schedule

  1. Home → Full schedule. Mon–Sun grid, 3 waste types, next date for each.
  2. The earliest date matches the Home board.

HP3. Sorting guide

Plan: visual waste-sorting guide

  1. Sort. 9 types with bin colours.
  2. Plastic. Orange bin, what goes in, what doesn't, tips, drop-off points.
  3. Search battery. Hazardous, "Keep separate", E-waste & battery bin.
  4. Also check Paper, E-waste and Food waste.

HP4. Drop-off locator (timed task 1)

Plan: drop-off locator; nearest drop-off point task

  1. Recycle. "I live in: Blok 6" and the map.
  2. Nearest is Community hall recycling bins, about 100 m.
  3. Tap the Plastic filter. Only places that take plastic.
  4. Open the first card. Location, distance, hours, accepts, sample note.
  5. Switch to Blok 3. Nearest becomes Recycling corner, about 40 m.

HP5. Report with a photo (timed task 2)

Plan: reporting form with photo, category, location

  1. Report → Illegal dumping → Blok 6 / Roadside → description.
  2. Add a photo (or Use a sample photo instead). Preview shows.
  3. Send report. "Report sent", WW-0108, Submitted.
  4. Track this report. Photo, Submitted in the timeline.
  5. Check: send with nothing filled in. The form says what's missing and doesn't send.

HP6. My reports

Plan: report details and statuses match

  1. My reports. WW-0108 and WW-0107 Submitted, WW-0104 Resolved.
  2. Open WW-0104. Handled by JMB maintenance team, timeline ends in Resolved.

HP7. Management view

Plan: admin view with Submitted / Under review / Resolved

  1. Gear → Open management view. Counts per status, WW-0108 with a photo.
  2. WW-0108 → assign JMB maintenance team → note → Mark under review.
  3. Mark resolved. "This report is resolved."
  4. Back → Resident app → My reports → WW-0108. Resolved, with your note.

HP8. Offline

Plan: technical disruptions backup

  1. Open the app once while online.
  2. Turn on airplane mode, reopen from the icon. Home, Sort, Recycle, Report all open.
  3. Send a report offline. It appears in My reports.

HP9. QR code

Plan: QR-code poster

  1. Show or print the QR code.
  2. Scan with an iPhone and an Android phone. The app opens with the "Add WasteWise to your home screen" page on top.

HP10. Reset between testers

Plan: usability testing

  1. Gear → Reset demo data → OK. "Demo data reset."
  2. My reports back to WW-0107 and WW-0104; management view back to 7 reports.

HP11. Language switch

Plan: Bahasa Melayu and English

  1. Tap BM on Home. Everything turns to BM; the button says EN.
  2. Sort → Plastik, Recycle, WW-0104. All in BM ("Selesai").
  3. Reload. Still BM. Tap EN to switch back.

HP12. Dark mode

Plan: text readability

  1. Gear → Dark mode on. Everything still readable.
  2. Reload. Still dark. Turn it off.

HP13. Add to Home Screen (required on phones)

Plan: QR on their own phones; offline

  1. Open the link in Safari or Chrome. The install page covers the app: an iPhone opens on the iPhone tab, an Android phone on the Android tab.
  2. Tap the other tab. That phone's steps and picture.
  3. Tap outside or swipe down. It stays: no close or "Not now"; the app behind can't be tapped.
  4. Tap BM. Steps in BM. Tap EN to switch back.
  5. Follow the steps, open WasteWise from the icon. No page on top; the app works.
  6. Open the link on a laptop. No page on top.
