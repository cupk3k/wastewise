# Usability test kit

For about 10 testers (convenience sample). One facilitator and, if possible, one note-taker per session. About 10 minutes per tester.

**You need:** a phone with the prototype open (the live link, or added to the home screen), a stopwatch (phone timer), the observation sheet below (printed or on a second phone), and the post-test questionnaire (Google Form link).

## Before each tester (pre-test reset)

1. On the test phone open **Settings** (gear icon, top right of Home), tap **Reset demo data**, then **OK**. This removes reports from the last tester and restores the sample reports. The app is set to **Blok 6** after a reset; leave it on Blok 6.
2. Go back to **Home**.
3. Have a photo ready on the phone (any picture of rubbish or a bin), or tell the tester they may use the "Use a sample photo instead" button.
4. Check the language and look: **English** (the button on Home says "BM") and dark mode off, unless the tester asks for Bahasa Melayu. Language and dark mode are not changed by the reset.
5. If an "Add WasteWise to your home screen" sheet covers the screen, tap **Not now** (or add the app to the home screen beforehand, then it never shows). Don't start the timer while it is open.
6. Start a new observation sheet.

## Facilitator script

Read this out, in your own words if you prefer:

> Hi, thank you for helping. We are testing an app idea for residents of Desa Mentari to sort waste, find recycling points and report waste problems. We are testing the app, not you, so there are no wrong answers. If something is confusing, that is useful for us to know.
>
> Everything in the app is pretend data. Nothing you type is sent to building management or anyone else.
>
> I will give you a few short tasks. Please think aloud as you go: say what you are looking for and what you expect to happen. I will time some tasks, but please go at your normal pace. I can't help during a task, but you can stop any time. Afterwards there is a short questionnaire.

**Consent line** (read out, and tick on the sheet when they agree):

> Taking part is voluntary and you can stop at any time. We will record how long tasks take and what you say, but not your name, phone number or face. The results are only used for our CSPH course project. Are you happy to continue?

Then for each task: read the task once, start the timer when you finish reading, stop it when the success point is reached (or the tester gives up, or 3 minutes pass). Don't hint. If the tester asks for help, say "What would you try?" and note it as an assist.

## Tasks

The tester starts each task from the **Home** screen. Go back to Home between tasks.

### Task 1 (timed): Find the nearest recycling drop-off point

> "You live in Blok 6 and have some plastic bottles to recycle. Find the nearest place you can drop them off, and tell me where it is."

- **Success:** the tester opens a drop-off point that accepts plastic and names it. With the sample data and Blok 6, the nearest is the **Community hall recycling bins** (about 100 m). Naming the Recycling corner (beside Blok 3) counts as completed but note it as an error: not the nearest.
- **Usual path:** Home → Drop-off points (or Guide → Plastic → drop-off point).
- **Stop the timer** when they name the place.

### Task 2 (timed): Submit a waste report with a photo

> "There is rubbish dumped by the roadside near your block. Report it, with a photo."

- **Success:** the "Report sent" screen appears with a reference number, and the report has a photo attached.
- **Usual path:** Home → Report a problem → choose a problem type → spot → description → add photo → Send.
- **Note as errors:** a wrong problem type (expected: Illegal dumping), sending without a photo, or the form stopping them because something was missed.
- **Stop the timer** when the Report sent screen appears.

### Task 3: Find what bin a battery goes in

> "You have some used batteries. Where should they go?"

- **Success:** the tester says batteries are kept separate (hazardous) and go to the **E-waste & battery bin** in the community hall lobby. Saying "not in the general bin" without naming the drop-off point counts as partly completed.
- **Usual path:** Guide → search "battery" (or Hazardous).

### Task 4: Check the next collection day

> "When is the next rubbish collection?"

- **Success:** the tester reads the day and type from the **Next collection** board on Home (or from the Schedule screen). Check the board yourself before the session; the answer depends on the day of the test.

### Task 5: Find a report's status

> "Find the report you just sent. What is its status? Then find your report about standing water at the playground. What is its status?"

- **Success:** the tester opens My reports and says the new report is **Submitted** and the standing-water report (WW-0104) is **Resolved**.
- **Usual path:** Home → Your open reports / See all, or the Reports tab.

## Post-test questionnaire (for a Google Form)

**Part A.** How much do you agree? (1 = Strongly disagree, 2 = Disagree, 3 = Neutral, 4 = Agree, 5 = Strongly agree)

1. **Navigation:** It was easy to find my way around the app.
2. **Readability:** The text was easy to read on the phone screen.
3. **Clarity:** The information (bins, drop-off points, schedule) was clear and easy to understand.
4. **Ease of reporting:** Reporting a waste problem was quick and easy.
5. **Visual hierarchy:** The most important information on each screen stood out.
6. **Overall satisfaction:** Overall, I am satisfied with the app.

**Part B.** Open questions (short answer). Ask these aloud first as a 2-minute post-test interview (the plan's qualitative feedback), and note the answers; then let the tester fill in the form.

7. What was the most useful part of the app for you?
8. What was confusing or difficult, if anything?
9. What one thing would you add or change so you would use it at home?

**Part C.** About you (optional, multiple choice)

10. Do you live in Desa Mentari? Yes / No / Prefer not to say
11. Which language would you prefer the app in? English / Bahasa Melayu / Chinese / Tamil / Other

## After all sessions

- Average time and completion rate for tasks 1 and 2.
- Count errors per task and list the most common ones.
- Average score for each Likert item.
- Group the open answers into themes and pick the top 3 changes.
